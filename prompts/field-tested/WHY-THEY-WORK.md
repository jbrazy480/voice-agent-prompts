# Why these patterns are useful

This collection illustrates short booking and human-transfer flows for medical practice, medspa, and dental conversations. The field-tested label is a collection name, not evidence of measured performance.

- Inbound calls begin with the caller's reason for contacting the business.
- Outbound calls identify the company and disclose the AI identity early.
- Confirm interest without pressure. Stop immediately on an opt-out.
- Offer concrete appointment windows only after checking availability.
- Transfer to a human when requested and available; otherwise ask permission for a callback.
- End silently on voicemail. Calling-platform status flags need review and are not proof of a completed conversation.

Adapt the variables, business facts, and calendar functions to your implementation. Keep the disclosure and opt-out instructions when shortening the script.
