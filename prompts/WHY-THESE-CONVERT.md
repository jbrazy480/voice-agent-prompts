# Choosing an industry prompt

Use the [index](README.md) to choose an industry and call type. Inbound prompts address a caller's request; outbound prompts should name the actual reason for contact. Reactivation prompts require a review of whether further contact is permitted.

Preserve the early AI disclosure, stop on opt-out, and offer a human when requested. Verify appointment availability before naming slots. End outbound calls silently on voicemail. These conversation patterns do not imply measured outcomes.

Replace business variables and tool placeholders with your verified configuration. Run `vap lint` after editing and test actual tool behavior in your calling platform.
